What does Digital Currency Inc. do?

Digital Currency Inc. helps independent artists and labels with music distribution, royalties, playlist strategy, sync preparation, video support, campaign planning, and label services.

Who is DCI built for?

DCI is built for independent artists, music labels, managers, producers, creators, and artist teams that want more support than basic upload-only distribution.

Yes. DCI connects music distribution with AI music tools, creator technology education, and guidance for artists and labels exploring modern release strategy.
