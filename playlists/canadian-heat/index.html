Canadian Hip-Hop

Canadian Heat

Canadian Heat is hip-hop with a northern stamp. It is for listeners who want Canadian rap that sounds current and local, not a coastal template with the names swapped. Tracks that fit are confident, rhythmic, and specific about where they are from. Generic rap with no point of view will not carry the shelf.
