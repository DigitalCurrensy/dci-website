Privacy Policy

Digital Currensy Inc. — Effective Date: June 16, 2026

Digital Currensy Inc. is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, legal name, stage name, email address, password, and profile preferences.
  • Asset Submissions: Audio files, metadata, artwork, lyrics, music videos, and social media links.
  • Financial Data: Tax information (W-9/W-8BEN), banking details, or PayPal routing data for royalty payouts.
  • Usage & Tech Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Delivering your content and metadata cleanly to streaming platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, etc.).
  • Processing, tracking, and distributing your royalty payments.

DCI does not sell your data. We share information only with essential partners required to perform our core distribution business:

Depending on your location (UK/EU under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you have the right to request access to your data, request corrections, or request total deletion. We retain your asset data for as long as your distribution agreement with DCI remains active.
